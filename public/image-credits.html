IMAGE AND FONT PROVENANCE

Asset credits & usage status.

This page separates original design work, AI-generated apparel concepts and third-party photographic sources. It is a transparency record, not a blanket copyright ownership claim.

Commercial asset clearance is still in progress.
Original generation does not automatically establish trademark rights, model releases, third-party photo permission, or a supplier's production capability. Verify all image licenses and source terms before launching the real business.

Homepage mannequin visuals

The homepage hero and four sector cards are AI-generated illustrative mannequin apparel concepts extracted from approved design source art and delivered by Cloudinary. They do not depict real orders, employees, factory premises or client brands. Their production record is maintained in the project's asset registry.

Virtual showroom garment concepts

The three completed four-color/three-angle school style sets are generated prototype images kept as versioned WebP files in the GitHub repository, and served through approved local or Cloudinary URLs. Other seeded garment thumbnails are concepts; color choices without photographs are quotation preferences. These images cannot substantiate merchandise availability or approved institutional logos.

Editorial manufacturing photographs

The production asset register identifies two supporting headless stock images from Unsplash: hands sewing fabric (attributed in the asset register to Erwin Bosman) and sewing equipment (attributed to Kevin Menya). Attribution details and license conditions must be rechecked against the original download records before commercial publication. No photo is presented as depicting Taytay Textile Co.'s own facility.

Typography

Site typography uses native/system font stacks: Georgia, Times New Roman, Segoe UI, Arial and Helvetica. No external font distribution file is bundled or served. Fonts are rendered by each visitor's browser and operating system under their respective local licenses.

Company and third-party marks

The site's original PH and Taytay Textile lettering is not an official Philippine government trustmark. No ISO, DTI, school, medical institution, customer or accreditation body logo is licensed or asserted by the garment illustrations. Any future mark requires documented permission or legal entitlement.

Correction requests and source verification

Before publication, the business operator should verify every commercial photo and mark against the provenance register and obtain necessary releases. For privacy-related image concerns, submit a formal privacy request. Other rights claims require the trading entity's verified contact information, which remains a launch gate.
