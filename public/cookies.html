Cookie & Storage Policy

An accurate inventory of the cookies and browser storage used by this version of the website.

Development policy · Operator review required
This site uses a provisional business identity and demonstration content. A complete legal-business disclosure and approved operating procedures are pending. Last revised 30 October 2026.

Strictly necessary account session

When you sign in, a secure, HttpOnly session cookie enables authentication, access separation and CSRF protection. You cannot turn this off while remaining signed in. The server sets session expiry. Logging out ends the active session.

Optional saved showroom brief

The virtual showroom can remember garment styles and colors on this browser using localStorage, but only if you enable “Save my project brief on this device” in Cookie Settings. With this off, saved styles remain temporary in the open tab and are not deliberately persisted between visits. Switching it off removes any prior locally stored brief.

Remembering this choice

A small on-device preference value remembers your choice about optional local saving. This is used only for privacy-preference management and does not identify an account or enable ads.

Security services and third-party requests

Cloudflare delivers hosting/security and may use necessary security cookies or technical signals. Cloudinary loads garment images and may receive request metadata. If the quote form is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded to protect submission. These services have their own policies. We have no configured advertising, retargeting, or optional site-analytics tracker in this build.

No misleading accept-all option

This site does not ask you to “accept advertising cookies” when there are no advertising cookies to enable. You may select Necessary only or allow optional local saving. Cookie Settings can be reopened at any time through the footer.

Managing storage

You may also remove the saved brief and preference from your browser storage through its settings. Clearing cookies logs you out. Browser-level privacy controls and extensions can affect site functionality.
