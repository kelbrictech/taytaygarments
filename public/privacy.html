PERSONAL DATA & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Notice

What information the demonstration website uses, why, and how to exercise your privacy rights.

Development policy · Operator review required
This site uses a provisional business identity and demonstration content. A complete legal-business disclosure and approved operating procedures are pending. Last revised 30 October 2026.

Status of this notice

This notice describes the development demonstration of Taytay Textile Co. and its currently implemented data flows. The trading entity’s confirmed legal name, business address, designated privacy contact and final records-retention schedule are pending operator verification. They must be finalized before production commercial launch. No claimed registration or certified status is implied.

Information submitted or collected

Quotation requests may collect the organization name and sector, contact name and email, garments, quantities, preferred delivery area, project notes and selected styles. Customer or staff accounts use names, emails, password-derived authentication material and session data. Staff approval records and order/quality events contain activity timestamps. Privacy-rights requests collect an email address, request type and any details you choose to add. We do not request government IDs or payment card numbers through these forms.

Purposes and lawful grounds

We use inquiry information to assess project requirements and respond; account information to authenticate users and restrict access; order and quality records to carry out requested services and record decisions; and rights-request details to process data-subject requests. Appropriate lawful bases may include consent, steps requested before a contract, contract performance, legal obligation or legitimate interests, as applicable. An unchecked quotation privacy acknowledgment must prevent submission. There is no separate marketing subscription or targeted-advertising system.

Service providers and data flows

Cloudflare hosts and secures the site and Worker APIs. Neon hosts its PostgreSQL application database. Cloudinary delivers garment images. When online inquiries are enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile may verify form submissions. These service providers may process limited technical data according to their service terms. Images are illustrative garment concepts or credited stock imagery; they do not establish a real factory or customer relationship.

Cookies and on-device storage

A necessary secure session cookie enables signed-in accounts. The public showroom can optionally save selected styles in this browser; this is disabled by default until you expressly enable it in Cookie Settings. Essential preference storage remembers your choice. We have not configured advertising pixels, behavior-based profiling or optional analytics in this build. Browser/network infrastructure may process necessary technical request data.

Access, security and transfers

Account information is scoped by user role. Server-managed sessions, CSRF checks, hashed passwords, input validation and protected staff dashboards provide technical safeguards. Service-provider systems can operate in locations outside the Philippines; applicable safeguards and contractual assessments need to be completed by the operating business. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Records should be retained only as needed for their stated purpose, applicable legal obligations and legitimate disputes. Final retention periods and secure backup-deletion procedures are awaiting documented management approval; the site cannot yet present them as finalized. You can submit access, correction, erasure/blocking, objection, withdrawal or portability requests through the request form below. Identity and legal-retention checks precede any alteration or deletion. An acknowledgment is not a promise that data has already been erased.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, rights include being informed, accessing data, objecting, rectification, erasure or blocking where applicable, portability, and lodging a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Some requests may require verification, and certain records may have to be retained where legally required.

Controller identification and contact status

Operating identity: “Taytay Textile Co.” is presently a provisional site brand. Confirmed legal seller name, exact registered business address, dedicated privacy officer contact, and responsible controller details have not been supplied to this deployment. Privacy requests can be filed through the secured on-site workflow, where a reference number is returned. Do not use demo contact details as verified addresses.
