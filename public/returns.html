Returns, Defects & Remedies

How nonconforming uniforms, workmanship issues, cancellations and approved samples should be handled.

Development policy · Operator review required
This site uses a provisional business identity and demonstration content. A complete legal-business disclosure and approved operating procedures are pending. Last revised 30 October 2026.

Current sales status

The public virtual showroom contains sample styles, not an operational checkout. There are no verified live sales conditions, refund timetable, payment processor or return depot published for this development site. The eventual trading operator must finalize these details before payment is accepted.

The written specification governs the order

For future made-to-order uniforms, the customer and supplier should approve the technical specification, measurements, fabric/shade sample, print or embroidery positions, artwork proofs, quantity, production milestone and acceptance criteria in writing. Record any authorized alterations and delivery commitments.

Defects and incorrect fulfillment

When garments are defective, materially different from the agreed sample/specification, short-delivered, or otherwise subject to a statutory remedy, the buyer may seek appropriate repair, replacement, refund or other remedies under applicable Philippine law. Customization or completed production does not remove mandatory rights for genuine defects.

Reporting an issue

Contact the real operator using the contact channel recorded in the confirmed order. Include the order reference, affected quantity, the agreed specification, a concise description of the problem and dated images where helpful. Never include passwords or payment-card details. Keep the original garments available for inspection where reasonable.

Review and resolution

The operator should acknowledge a complaint, assess the goods against approved specifications and the law, document the findings, and propose a legally appropriate remedy. Any return delivery arrangement, cost allocation or refund timing must follow applicable law and the confirmed order rather than an invented blanket deadline.

Changes of mind and approved custom work

A mere change of mind about goods matching their agreed specification may be treated differently from a defect. For customized work, cancellation stages, already-approved expenses and rework arrangements should be agreed before production. Any such agreement remains subject to non-waivable consumer rights.

What we will not claim

This website does not impose a blanket “No Return, No Exchange” policy, guarantee refunds within a particular number of days without an approved procedure, or use seeded demonstration orders as proof of a real remedy process.

Operator details pending approval

The responsible seller's legal name, customer-service contact, physical return location and approved refund processing procedure have not yet been verified. Those are mandatory launch decisions before accepting customer funds.
