Site and operator status

Taytay Textile Co. has operated since 2018. This website presents garment style references and a project-inquiry journey. The seller's full legal identity, verified business address and contact channels must be stated on the written quotation and order confirmation. A showroom selection or inquiry does not itself establish a sale or accepted order.

Browsing and quotations

Garment images are for style reference and do not guarantee stock. Color selectors indicate preferences unless the corresponding approved image exists. A saved project brief or online inquiry acknowledgment does not create a sale, reserve inventory, establish a price or guarantee production capacity.

Confirming a custom order

Any future binding garment order should have an approved written quotation and specification covering the garment description, quantity, fabric, approved colors and samples, sizing, unit prices and taxes, payment terms, delivery and inspection terms, expected timeline, revision process and contact information of the actual seller. Changes after approval need mutual written agreement.

Payment and fulfillment

This website does not currently collect online payments or provide checkout. Production and delivery are not guaranteed until the seller accepts a written order with agreed specifications, payment terms and delivery arrangements. Verify payment instructions directly with the named seller before transferring funds.

Customer accounts and security

Account credentials are issued by the operator; do not share passwords. Order visibility is limited to the authenticated customer's linked records. Staff access requires administrative approval. You may request access or correction through the Privacy Notice and its request form.

Content and rights

Consumer rights and complaints

Nothing in these terms waives statutory consumer protections. Eligible consumers retain remedies under Philippine law, including when goods are defective or fail to match agreed descriptions. Concerns should be documented with the actual order and supporting specification. See Returns & Remedies for the review process.

Law and changes

Applicable Philippine laws may apply depending on the transaction and customer. Material revisions should be posted with an effective date and, for confirmed customers, communicated where required. A site policy cannot override mandatory statutory rights. These terms and the seller details should be reviewed and approved before the website is used to accept binding commercial orders.
