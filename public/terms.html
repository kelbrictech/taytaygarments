Terms & Conditions

Clear boundaries for garment concepts, quotation requests, user accounts and confirmed orders.

Development policy · Operator review required
This site uses a provisional business identity and demonstration content. A complete legal-business disclosure and approved operating procedures are pending. Last revised 30 October 2026.

Site and operator status

This site is a development demonstration under the provisional brand Taytay Textile Co. The exact trading entity, verified address, contact channels, licensing and ability to accept live orders must be confirmed before commercial launch. The virtual showroom and seeded customer orders are demonstrations unless explicitly identified as verified live work.

Browsing and quotations

Photorealistic mannequin renders and other garment images are concept references, not photographs of fulfilled orders or guaranteed stock. Color selectors indicate preferences unless the corresponding approved image exists. A saved project brief or online inquiry acknowledgment does not create a sale, reserve inventory, establish a price or guarantee production capacity.

Confirming a custom order

Any future binding garment order should have an approved written quotation and specification covering the garment description, quantity, fabric, approved colors and samples, sizing, unit prices and taxes, payment terms, delivery and inspection terms, expected timeline, revision process and contact information of the actual seller. Changes after approval need mutual written agreement.

Payment and fulfillment

This development site does not currently collect card payments, operate a checkout, or guarantee production/delivery. Do not transfer money based solely on a sample quotation or demo dashboard. The actual supplier must confirm payment instructions, order acceptance, and shipping arrangements through independently verified channels.

Customer accounts and security

Account credentials are issued by the operator; do not share passwords. Order visibility is limited to the authenticated customer's linked records. Staff access requires administrative approval. You may request access or correction through the Privacy Notice and its request form.

Content and rights

Branding, layouts and source text are site materials; some illustrative garments and images are generated or third-party stock content. Third-party licensing, attribution and rights must be verified by the business before commercial publication. Do not copy third-party marks or claim affiliation with named institutions from fictional samples.

Consumer rights and complaints

Nothing in these terms waives statutory consumer protections. Eligible consumers retain remedies under Philippine law, including when goods are defective or fail to match agreed descriptions. Concerns should be documented with the actual order and supporting specification. See Returns & Remedies for the review process.

Law and changes

Applicable Philippine laws may apply depending on the transaction and customer. Material revisions should be posted with an effective date and, for confirmed customers, communicated where required. A site policy cannot override mandatory statutory rights. These terms need final legal and management approval before real commercial selling.
