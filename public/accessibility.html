ACCESS FOR EVERY USER

Accessibility Statement

Accessibility design commitments, current safeguards and an honest account of what remains untested.

Development policy · Operator review required
This site uses a provisional business identity and demonstration content. A complete legal-business disclosure and approved operating procedures are pending. Last revised 30 October 2026.

Target and current status

Our engineering target is the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This development site has not completed a qualified independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim audited WCAG AA conformance.

Current usability safeguards

The site uses responsive layouts, semantic headings, labeled form fields, visible keyboard focus, meaningful text alternatives for product photos, identifiable controls and status messages. Public legal pages avoid image-only text and are designed for keyboard and mobile use.

Known areas for testing

The modal showroom, screen-reader announcements, complex staff dashboards, all dynamic form errors, touch target sizes, color contrast in every state and zoom/text-spacing behavior require additional assistive-technology and manual testing. Automated scans alone cannot verify complete accessibility.

Help and feedback

For access problems, describe the affected page and controls without including sensitive information. Use the site's public contact/quotation channel while the operator confirms an accessibility support address. We will document fixes as part of the ongoing development work.

Practical workarounds

You can enlarge text using browser zoom, navigate links and form fields with the Tab key, and use keyboard Enter or Space to activate standard buttons. If a form does not work for your setup, record the issue and request an accessible alternative from the operator before relying on a commercial transaction.
