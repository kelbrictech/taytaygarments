# ISO 9001:2026 readiness and QMS implementation — Taytay Textile Co.

**Prepared:** 2026-10-28
**Evidence status:** Software implementation in progress. No organizational conformity or independent ISO 9001 certification has been verified.

## Source basis and limits

ISO 9001:2026 (sixth edition) was published on September 16, 2026. It supersedes ISO 9001:2015 and the 2024 climate-action amendment. The 2026 edition retains the harmonized management-system structure and emphasizes leadership, ethical conduct and quality culture, strategic alignment, risks and opportunities as distinct considerations, and continuity of climate-context analysis. Annex A gives interpretive guidance.

The standard is copyrighted. This document independently paraphrases implementation objectives rather than reproducing the normative clauses. For formal conformity assessment, obtain an authorized copy and engage qualified management and audit personnel.

## QMS software delivered

- Versioned documented information: drafts, reviews, independent administrator approval, immutability after approval, history of revisions.
- Separately categorized risks, opportunities, nonconformities, customer complaints and corrective actions; closure requires root cause, corrective action and effectiveness review.
- Immutable quality records for order requirements, supplier assessments, incoming and in-process inspections, final inspection, competence, internal audit, management review, feedback, controlled change, objectives and climate relevance.
- A new dispatch/delivery status update requires a traceable release note and the **latest final-inspection result to be PASS**, linked to the same order and demo/live classification.
- Access governed by approved session and CSRF: customers/webmasters cannot enter the quality management workspace; staff can create operational evidence; administrators alone approve controlled documents and close verified corrective actions.
- Public Quality page explains quality process intentions and explicitly discloses that certification is unverified. No ISO corporate logo, certification logo or claim of conformity is displayed.
- The existing Trust & Verification ISO 9001 registry remains evidence-required.

## Demo seed status

Five policy/procedure/scope documents were seeded as unapproved illustrative drafts. Three fictional risk/opportunity/nonconformity cases were seeded as open. No physical quality inspection records were fabricated. The existing orders may have historical statuses from before the release gate was created: those are **not retroactively quality approved**.

The company's ISO 9001 trust badge is currently in research status. Do not label Taytay Textile Co. ISO-certified, ISO-compliant, ISO-approved, or use the ISO logo based on any of these records.

## Operational action plan for the client

1. Confirm the legal business entity and facilities. Set a real QMS scope with services, outsourced processes, interested parties, relevant environmental/climate factors and any justified exclusions/nonapplicability.
2. Assign accountable management, a quality owner, technical approvers, competent inspectors and independent internal auditors. Define employee ethics and escalation standards.
3. Review and replace the seeded draft quality policy and scope using real management input. Document approval by a separate authorized individual; communicate approved information to every relevant role.
4. Build the garment process map: customer request, approved tech pack, sourcing, supplier control, lot traceability, cutting, sewing, in-process QC, finishing, final QC, disposition and dispatch.
5. Run a real order pilot with approved measurements, color/sample, materials, work instructions, inspection plan, actual readings, release decision and traceable evidence. Do not record a PASS without a real assessment.
6. Record baseline and target KPIs: first-pass yield, reject rate, sewing defects, on-time shipment, customer complaints, supplier rejections and corrective-action effectiveness. Targets must be approved by management.
7. Train staff and verify competence against their actual assigned work. Implement equipment maintenance, calibration plans and storage/handling controls.
8. Record real NC/complaint investigations, containment, root causes, changes and verified effectiveness. Audit records should be append-only and linked to actual cases.
9. Perform an impartial internal audit and an actual management review with resources, customer satisfaction, KPI results, risks and improvement actions.
10. Purchase the official ISO 9001:2026 normative text, conduct an evidence-based gap review and seek an independent accredited certification body only after real operation of the QMS.

## Current system limitations requiring decisions

The QMS register stores text and evidence references. It does not yet upload signed buyer-approved tech packs, authenticate calibration certificates, independently verify audit evidence, enforce data retention rules, provide MFA, or automate independent assessor permissions. A final-inspection PASS can only represent trustworthy evidence when the authorized inspector is competent and the referenced measurements/specifications are real and retained. Do not use the demo accounts for actual production release.

The system documents accountable actions and can block uninspected software dispatch transitions. Physical manufacturing compliance, safety rules, legal registrations, QMS implementation and external certification remain the company's responsibility.
